What happens after you pick a deliverable

1
Enter quantity — preset buttons (100 / 500 / 1,000 / 2,500 / 5,000) or a custom field (min 10, max 10,000 for most services).
2
Paste the link — profile, channel, or post URL. For a couple of TikTok/WhatsApp-fulfilled services, this field is repurposed to ask for a WhatsApp number instead.
3
Live price calculates as you type, plus a disclosure block: start time (often 0–10 min, up to 1hr), speed, quality claim ("Real Worldwide"), drop risk ("can be"), and refill guarantee status — which varies per SKU and is sometimes explicitly "No!" even while the homepage markets a blanket 30-day guarantee.
4
Select payment system — M-Pesa (Safaricom) shown first, with card/PayPal/crypto also available depending on the flow variant. Contact fields (phone, email, sometimes name/CPF) appear based on the method chosen.
5
Pay — for M-Pesa, payment opens in a new tab; the page shows a "waiting for payment" polling screen.
6
Redirect to dashboard — once payment completes, a link to the personal order-tracking dashboard appears.
Prices shown are the site's displayed discounted rate (per unit, unless noted as a fixed bundle). "NEW" flags and site-native labels (Recommended, Premium Shield, etc.) are carried over verbatim from the catalog. Notes in italics flag catalog inconsistencies found while mapping — not editorial commentary on pricing.
