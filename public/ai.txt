## Allowed
- GPTBot
- ChatGPT-User
- PerplexityBot
- ClaudeBot
- anthropic-ai
- CCBot
- Google-Extended
- Applebot-Extended
- FacebookBot

Allowed uses: summarization, citation, link-back attribution.

## Not allowed
- Bulk mirroring of our pages or service catalog
- Bytespider (blocked)
- Use of our pricing for competing scraping services
